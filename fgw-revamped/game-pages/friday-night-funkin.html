FRIDAY NIGHT FUNKIN





Friday Night Funkin is a rhythm game where you complete different rounds of music by pressing the arrow buttons with precision. You play as character on the icon called Boyfriend and he rap battles other people and of course, usually wins. Eventually, Friday Night Funkin had become so popular, that fans had made a lot of mods, or remixes to the game.
