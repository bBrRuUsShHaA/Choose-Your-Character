HATCHIMALS COLLEGGTIBLES





Hatchimals Colleggtibles is a mobile game where you hatch different animals with wings and build your hatchimal collection. There's loads of different places to go in the land of Hatchtopia, where all the Hatchimals live. In some parts, there's also minigames where you can collect different currencies and even more eggs to hatch. With different currencies that depend on which minigame you're playing, you can also buy hatchimals from the Hatchy Basket (I had all of them 😎). Also, along with hatching hatchimals, it's also your responsibility to take care of them, like feeding them, bathing them, and making them happy. Hatchimals also have their own favorite foods. Unfortunately, this game is not functioning anymore and is no longer in the app store 😭
