SONIC DASH





Sonic Dash is a mobile game which is in the endless runner category, a category where you play as a character running endlessly dodging obstacles and gaining powerups, and there's also high scores for how long you run. Overtime you can collect character cards to unlock more characters the more you start playing. The main currency for the game are your classic rings which can be used to upgrade your characters. There are also red rings which are more rare to get and don't come up often, and they're mostly there to continue the game once you crash (if you have enough of them), and finally there's gems to use for rebuilding habitats for the animals you collect by destroying Badniks. There are also boss battles you can go through, and the only two bosses are Zazz, a member of the Deadly Six, and the nefarious Dr. Eggman. Once you successfully defeat them, you get a hefty reward.
