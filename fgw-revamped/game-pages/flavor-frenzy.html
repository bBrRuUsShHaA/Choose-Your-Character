FLAVOR FRENZY





Flavor Frenzy is a 3D Tower Defense game where you build and level up your army of cute bears (that are armed and/or magical) to defend your base. There's the main mode where you can free play with other people, and there's also Story Mode where you slowly find out the lore of the game. In story mode, there's an infection that's turning all of the animals green and hostile, and even other bears aren't safe from the infection. The main characters are Sushi, Pepper, and Cupcake, who later meet Flan and Ube, which the latter also introduces him to his best friend Mocha. Unfortunately, expect no updates from this game, as this game has not been worked on and not anytime soon, which means we won't have all of Story Mode. But a bonus fact is that the game is made by the same creator of Dandy's World.
