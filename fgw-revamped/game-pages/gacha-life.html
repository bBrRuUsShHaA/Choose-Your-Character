GACHA LIFE





Gacha Life is a game where you can create your own characters and roleplay with them or to create your own stories with Studio Mode. There are also minigames you can play along with the Life Mode where you can take your character to any terrain and have it make new friends. In the Gacha section, you can get 1 or 50 random presents, depending on how much gems you spend, to get more stamina and also different presents that you can give as a gift to characters to improve their friendship. I like games where you can make your own characters and roleplay with them so don't blame me for liking Gacha Life.
