DANDY'S WORLD





Dandy's World is a 3D indie horror game where you play as 1 out of 39 playable characters called Toons (2 are developer-exclusive, which means 41 total) descend down the elevator to fill up all ichor machines on a floor to open up the elevator and descend further. But there are also warped versions of the Toons, called Twisteds, as they have been infected with a black substance called ichor. There are few different types of Twisteds, which are roaming Twisteds which are the most common, stationary Twisteds, and ranged Twisteds, and like the Toons, they have their own rarities. Along with this, there's also a Roleplay Mode you can join in the lobby, where you can roleplay as all 41 Toons and even their Twisted counterparts. As you complete filling up ichor machines, you get ichor as currency where you can spend at Dandy's store to buy more Toons, or at Dyle's Store to buy skins, with both of them being in the lobby. There's also a shop in the elevator that'll appear every even floor called Dandy's Shop, where you can buy items that'll help you along the way, for the cost of Tapes that can be collected by picking them up when they're scattered all over the floor, along with other items, like research capsules that will give you 1% research of a Twisted on the current floor (You also get research by encountering it and you get double research if you're playing as Rodger), and bandages/medkits that'll replenish one or all of your hearts. Each Toon has their own stats and one ability, whether passive or active, and Main Toons have a passive and active ability, but they only have two hearts instead of three hearts. An exception to this is Soulvester, a Halloween-exclusive Toon, who is not a Main Toon but has four hearts. Over the course of the year, there are also events that happen in a certain period of time where you can get Event-exclusive Toons and skins, and even Event-exclusive Elevator floor and Floor Events, Twisteds, and currencies. For example, the Christmas Event has an exclusive kind of Floor Event called an Iced Over floor, where the ground is completely icy which causes Toons and Twisteds to slide around. There are also stickers that you can also purchase in Dyle's Shop to communicate with your teammates, and badges you can collect by completing quests.
