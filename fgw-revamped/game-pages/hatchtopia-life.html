HATCHTOPIA LIFE





Hatchtopia Life is a game that's similar to Hatchimals Colleggtibles, but it's in another toy line called Hatchtopia Life, where instead of the hatchimals being plastic, they're now plushies and have bean bags inside them (I have 5). In this mobile game, you don't have to take care of your hatchimals' needs, and it doesn't take time for the eggs to hatch, and a way to collect eggs are to scan the QR code that comes with a real Hatchtopia Life toy. You can also give your hatchimals accessories and even build and upgrade homes for them (along with customizing the inside of their home), essentially making your own little town. Like Hatchimals Colleggtibles, there are minigames that give you prizes. Sadly, this game is also not functioning and has been removed from the app store.
