MARIO KART TOUR





Mario Kart Tour is a part of the Mario Kart series, and the only Mario Kart that's a mobile game. The difference is that you can race against other people from all around the world and gain higher tiers to show off your racing skills! You control your driver with your finger and can even hold your drifts longer to get a little faster boost like other Mario Kart games. You can also collect stamps by completing quests, and you can even get more rewards by clearing a whole horizontal, vertical, or diagonal line just like in a bingo board. When you get all stamps, you get a super reward. Like some other Mario Kart games, you can also play as Miis! There's a lot of different suits they can wear as well.
