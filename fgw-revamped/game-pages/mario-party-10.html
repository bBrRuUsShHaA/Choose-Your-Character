MARIO PARTY 10





Mario Party 10 is one of the games from the Mario Party series, and just like any other Mario Party game, the goal is to get as many stars and coins as you can get by winning mini games and landing on good spaces when rolling the dice (or die). A new feature they added for this game are two different modes. There's your regular Mario Party but they replaced Super Stars with Mini Stars, and there's also Bowser Party. This mode can be played with five players, with four players being Mario characters (Team Mario who play with joy-cons), and one being Bowser (Team Bowser who plays with the Wii pad). The goal for Team Mario is to get to the end of the course before Bowser, and the goal for Team Bowser is to eliminate all players on Team Mario, and to do so is to catch up to where they are and force them to play one of Bowser's minigames which the player being Bowser can control.
