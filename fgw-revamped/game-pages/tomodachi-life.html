TOMODACHI LIFE: LIVING THE DREAM





Tomodachi Life: Living the Dream is also the type of game where you can create your own characters and roleplay. But unlike Gacha Life, Tomodachi LIfe: Living the Dream goes more in-depth. For example, like Hatchtopia Life, you can build your own village, in this case an island, and construct houses for your Miis, but in this case they're automatically built for each one you make. Your characters can also build relationships with each other, like friends, family ties, or even falling in love with each other and getting married (and yes they can have kids together). Miis also have likes and dislikes, which is similar to Hatchimals Colleggtibles.
