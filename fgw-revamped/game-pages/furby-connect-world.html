FURBY CONNECT WORLD





Furby Connect World is a game where the main goal is to hatch Furby eggs and collect all the baby Furbys, called Furblets. Like Hatchimals Colleggtibles, it's your responsibility to take care ALL of your Furblets, ALL OF THEM, and there's about forty or more Furblets total, and yes you can hatch duplicates. But instead of needing to make your Furblets happy, your Furblets will become sick, so you'll have to treat them as well. But over time, the work pays off, because if you take care of your Furblets for long enough, they'll level up and give you gifts. Over time, you'd recieve Furby eggs that eventually, when the icon is fully purple, will activate the egg machine, where it'll give you a random egg. Another feature about this app is that if you have a real life Furby Connect (I have a coral one named Noo-boh) along with bluetooth, you can connect your Furby Connect to the app to do more things with it, such as feeding it with a food cannon, watching tv with it on the big screen, and even helping it take a dump, the #2, the furby feces. But just like with Hatchimals Colleggtibles and Hatchtopia Life, Furby Connect World is no longer working nor available in the app store.
