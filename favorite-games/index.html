MY FAVORITE GAMES

Introduction

People love all kinds of games, and everyone has their own taste. Some people like action games, cute games, horror games, and everything in between. Some people like some games because they're good at them, and other people like other games because of the lore. Either way, people have their own favorite games, and that's their own opinion. And my favorite games are my own opinion. This is a list of my favorite games:

  • Flavor Frenzy
  • Dandy's World
  • Friday Night Funkin'
  • Geometry Dash
  • Furby Connect World
  • Mario Party 10
  • Tomodachi Life
  • Sonic Dash

Why I like Flavor Frenzy

Before I tell you why, I need to tell you what Flavor Frenzy is. Flavor Frenzy is a tower defense game where a trio of cute bears fight off a strange green infection that's taking over the animals. They have to defeat all the infected animals with their weapons and powers, meet new bears with different quirks, and even defeat infected bears. But sadly, the Story Mode was never finished. This is an instance where I love the game only because of the characters. The thing is, I'm not a fan of tower defense games at all, but I love all the bears' designs which is why I love the game and why I think I'd have fun playing.

Why I really really love Dandy's World

Oh boy, this is gonna be a long paragraph. So what even is Dandy's World? Well, for one thing, Dandy's World and Flavor Frenzy are made by the same creator, which is how I even knew about Flavor Frenzy. Dandy's World in a Nutshell is that it's a horror game where you play as one of the thirty-nine playable Toons (41 Toons in total) who's quest is to descend through the floors and get to the highest floor possible, and the way to do that is by completing what are called ichor machines that open the elevator door once they're all completed. But there are also infected and hostile versions of these Toons called Twisteds which roam around the floor, and if you make contact with them, they'll chase after you and kill you. There are different kinds of Twisteds, like roaming Twisteds, the most common ones, ranged Twisteds which can attack or get to you from afar, and stationary Twisteds which don't move at all, but if you make contact with them, they can still kill you. There are also differnet rarities of Twisteds, along with different rarities for Toons, with Common being the lowest, and Lethal being the highest and most dangerous. So far, there are only two Lethals in the game, Dandy and Dyle, which are the only two Toons that are not playable in the game (unless if you're a developer). Dandy is the main character of Dandy's World and also the mascot of his own show, while Dyle is the one that operates all the trains through where all the Toons live which is Gardenview Center, which means he provides all items and necessities to Gardenview which keeps it running, but he is also the only Toon who was never in the show. The Lethal Duo can both be found in the Lobby in their own shop where the player would spawn, and their Twisteds can also be found while the player is going on a run. Players can either go on solo runs or with teams, because in Dandy's World, teamwork makes the dream work. Each playable Toon (or should I say each playable Toon besides ONE) has at least one ability that would aid themself or others, while Toons in the Main Character rarity have two abilities, but only have two hearts, as opposed to three hearts like the rest of the Toons. Unlike Flavor Frenzy, I actually played Dandy's World a couple of times, and I really enjoyed it, although my highest floor was only Floor 2. (Curse you Baby Wipes...)

Why I like Friday Night Funkin' (along with Geometry Dash)

I'm putting Friday Night Funkin' and Geometry Dash in the same section because they're both rhythm games. Friday Night Funkin' is a rhythm game where you play as a character named Boyfriend (yes that's his name) and he rap battles a series of characters like his girlfriend's parents, his ex, and even a lemon demon. To win the rap battle, you must press the arrow keys (or arrow buttons if you're on mobile) at the right time for at least most of the song. Meanwhile, Geometry Dash is a game where you play as a block that jumps over lots of obstacles by you pressing the space key, the up arrow, or the touchpad, and the best strategy to jump over the spikes and obstacles without getting killed is to jump in the rhythm in the song. There are also items that will help you stay in the rhythm like springs, purple springs which makes you bounce lower than a regular spring, anti-gravity springs, and even portals that switch the side of the screen or which transports the block in a rocket ship. I'm actually a fan of rhythm games which is another reason I put both these games in the same section, and I think it's fun to play and I like the songs.

Why I like Furby Connect World

Furby Connect World is a game where you hatch baby Furbys called Furblets and collect them all. Your job is to feed, bathe, and take care of them when they're sick. Another feature the app had was that if you had a Furby Connect, you could connect it to the app and do more things with it. Unfortunately, the app in no longer playable, but I still enjoyed playing it with my Furby Connect.

Why I like Tomodachi Life

Tomodachi Life is a game where you can customize characters and have them interact together in a mini world of your creation, and you control that world. I always love games where you can make your own characters and have them go through life in your own village that you made, and even though I never played it, I think it would be fun to. Plus, I already have a Nintendo Switch, so I think I could play it.

Why I like Mario Party 10

There are multiple Mario Party games ever created, like my second favorite Mario Party game, like Mario Party Superstars, and even the most recent Mario Party games like Mario Party Jamboree. But my favorite Mario Party game is Mario Party 10. It's because it has a special mode called Bowser Party, where one player, who has the Wii pad, plays as Bowser, and the other three players work together to win instead of Bowser. But, Bowser in my opinion is way more powerful than the other players, which is why I always call dibs on Bowser in Bowser Party.

Why I like Sonic Dash

Sonic Dash is an endless runner game, like other typical endless runner games like Temple Run and Subway Surfers. But the reason why I like this more than the other two combined is that I had more fun playing it, and it was Sonic, something that I'm...ahem....far too familiar with.
